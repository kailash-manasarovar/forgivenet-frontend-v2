Quick Start

Welcome to forgivenet, the world's first spiritual tech.

This guide will help you make your first forgiveness request on the blockchain.

1. Get a wallet

You need an Ethereum-compatible wallet to interact with forgivenet. If you don't already have one, you can get started with either of the following:

  • MetaMask
  • Coinbase Wallet

Install your chosen wallet and follow its instructions to set it up. Keep your recovery phrase private and never share it with anyone.

2. Select the Sepolia test network

forgivenet is currently being tested on Sepolia, an Ethereum test network. Select Sepolia in your wallet before proceeding.

Sepolia uses test ETH rather than real ETH. Test ETH has no monetary value and is used to test transactions without spending real cryptocurrency.

3. Get some test ETH

You need Sepolia test ETH to pay for your transaction and the donation amount you enter on forgivenet.

You can get Sepolia test ETH from the QuickNode Sepolia faucet . Select Ethereum and Sepolia, then enter your wallet address or connect your wallet. Follow the instructions to request your test ETH.

Make sure you request the funds for the same wallet address you intend to connect to forgivenet. Your Ethereum address is the same on Sepolia and Ethereum Mainnet, but the balances are separate.

4. Connect your wallet

Return to the forgivenet home page and click Connect Wallet.

Choose your wallet from the available options and approve the connection. Make sure the displayed network is Sepolia before continuing.

5. Submit your forgiveness request

Write your forgiveness request in the text box. Your request must contain between 501 and 1,999 characters.

Enter your donation amount in ETH. The current minimum is 0.000001 ETH.

When you're ready, click Send Request and review the transaction in your wallet. Check that you're using Sepolia and that the transaction details are correct before approving it.

Once submitted, your transaction can be viewed on the Sepolia blockchain. Your request may also appear in the forgivenet Record.

A final note

Blockchain transactions are public and generally irreversible. Avoid including personal information or anything you would not want publicly accessible. While forgivenet is on Sepolia, you are using test ETH rather than real funds.

Thank you for being part of forgivenet.
